## Coordinator decisions and safety

- **N1:** Executable grants for running processes use the OS-attested image. Existing GOAT grant tests remain; upstream attested-image test is retained. Other platforms adopt the upstream attested-path rule. Launch authorization stays distinct.

This is source analysis, not a live reproduction.
- **N3:** The Windows foreground route waits for the target queue's activation, rechecks exact foreground before the focus callback and again after the later wait, leaves a foreign foreground in place during cleanup, signals the outer browser restore owner, and services sent messages while attached. The browser setup restore makes only one rechecked attempt, bound to the original HWND and owner PID, without assisted retry or extra key injection; the foreground read and OS restore are not atomic. `InputQueueAttachment` is constructed only after a successful attach and detaches on `Drop`; GOAT partial-input and no-resend outcomes remain. The cross-platform admission, restore-callback and post-focus uncertainty tests `changed_or_unbound_foreground_refuses_after_wait`, `restoration_never_displaces_a_foreign_foreground`, `failed_wait_after_focus_is_never_reported_as_not_delivered`, `failed_attachment_never_constructs_a_detach_guard`, `a_failed_wait_after_focus_never_restores_over_unread_or_foreign_input`, `foreign_foreground_failure_survives_to_the_outer_restore_owner` and `unbound_same_hwnd_is_not_reported_as_a_foreign_window` and `setup_restore_rechecks_foreground_before_issuing_restore` failed first and then passed; none executes Win32. Post-focus wait failures are non-retryable uncertainty because focus may already have sent input; they retain the target or foreign foreground rather than restoring while delivery is unproven.
- **N4:** Sheet ancestry must be proven clear for token-addressed and window-addressed point clicks; the sheet-specific AXChildren reader treats a failed, absent, malformed or wrong-type result as unknown. A sheet appearing after admission is checked again at AX press, selection/focus, simulated pointer and multi-click dispatch. Where activation or prior input may have happened, the result does not claim nothing happened. `sheet_child_array_with_non_element_is_unknown`, `sheet_opening_before_dispatch_refuses_axopen_and_pointer`, `a_blocked_or_unreadable_sheet_never_dispatches_a_point`, `a_sheet_refusal_after_pointer_preparation_is_unverifiable`, `sheet_block_stops_the_focus_ladder_and_preserves_prior_input`, `selection_pixel_refuses_a_sheet_or_changed_point_before_dispatch`, `pre_target_check_stops_the_target_after_preparation`, `uncertain_selection_preparation_is_publicly_unverifiable` and `selection_foreground_requires_a_readable_focused_window` failed first then passed. The selection-point test also failed first and passed for a lost foreground. Window-addressed points now refuse when their AX hit or sheet ancestry is unreadable. Renderer target IDs scope semantic reads and continuations; store lookup continues to use the trusted caller session. DOM confirmation is evidence only, not a re-click; the original target is rechecked at each yielded boundary and detached targets do not earn page or target-readback confirmation. A failed CDP reply, JavaScript exception or unproven target connection yields an unverifiable action with no retry escalation and a do-not-retry summary, because the asynchronous sequence may have acted or may continue.
